About

I am an undergraduate student at Peking University, School of Electronics Engineering and Computer Science (EECS).

My interest is in Vision-and-Language Navigation (VLN) — building embodied agents that follow natural-language instructions through 3D environments by grounding language in what they perceive. More broadly I care about computer vision: how visual representations are learned, and what they actually capture.

I am always happy to talk about research, reading lists, or collaboration ideas — the fastest way to reach me is email.
